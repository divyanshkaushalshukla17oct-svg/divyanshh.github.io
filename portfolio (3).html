Chapters one to five, from a school club to a classroom in Kheri

Why hand a 19-year-old a go-to-market plan?

Fair question. I kept saying yes to the next slightly bigger job, and then finishing it. Here are the jobs, in order.

01

City Montessori School

Club Head, Storytelling Club

I organised the events and mentored the newer members. Most of marketing turned out to be the same job: get people to care about something, then step aside.

02

Student Council

Host, Chief Justice Conference and Celesta

I hosted the International Chief Justice Conference and Celesta, live. A stumble on stage can’t be edited out afterwards, which is decent preparation for any presentation.

03

Starbucks franchise outlet, Lucknow

Social media and in-store events

I ran the outlet’s Instagram. The tricky part was sounding like Lucknow without drifting from a global brand. I planned the engagement pushes, hosted jamming sessions and invited local influencers.

I have no follower figure to quote, so I won’t invent one.

“If you don’t like what’s being said, change the conversation.”Don Draper, Mad Men

04

Trivedi Agencies (TVS), 1 May to 30 Jul 2025

Marketing Intern

Three months on the dealership’s social media, promotions and events. The flyers, posters and banners were mine to design. The owner’s signed letter is in the proof section.

05

Aira Shiksha Seva Samiti, 1 Aug 2025 to 28 Feb 2026

Teaching volunteer

I taught foundational literacy and numeracy to underprivileged children, then helped raise funds for their schooling. It earns a place here because explaining something simply, to someone with every right to be bored, is most of the job. Certificate.

Mishra Enterprise, Khamaria, 1 May 2025 to 1 May 2026

The ₹3 Cr plan

certificate says 270%. I did the sums: 267%.

₹3 CrBefore
₹11 CrFY2025–26, up 267%

Starting point

Turnover of ₹3 Cr, and a business that needed customers it wasn’t yet reaching.

My part

I began with market research to find the segments nobody was serving. Then I designed the go-to-market plan and ran it alongside the leadership.

Where it landed

₹11 Cr in FY2025–26, a rise of 267%. See the certificate.

Also in the toolkit: Canva for flyers, posters and banners, a content calendar for every account, and my own Instagram page at 3,000 followers. Languages: English, Hindi and basic German.
